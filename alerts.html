Alert Level Guide

Understanding what each alert level means.

Normal

Normal Conditions

Temperatures are within normal seasonal conditions. Continue normal activities.

Watch

Heat Being Monitored

Temperatures are elevated. Stay hydrated and monitor local updates.

Warning

High Heat Risk

Limit prolonged outdoor activity and take additional precautions.

Severe

Extreme Heat Risk

Avoid unnecessary outdoor exposure and follow official heatwave safety instructions.


What Should You Do?

During High or Severe Heat Alerts

  • Drink water regularly, even when you do not feel thirsty.
  • Avoid unnecessary outdoor activity during the hottest part of the day.
  • Wear lightweight and breathable clothing.
  • Check on children, older adults and people who may be vulnerable to extreme heat.
  • Follow local government and emergency service announcements.

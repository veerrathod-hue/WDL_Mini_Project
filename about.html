About this project

Use case KJS-CES-01: Climate Intelligence for Heatwave Monitoring, Prediction, and Early Warning, with the India Meteorological Department (Mumbai-Pune).

The problem

Heatwaves are getting more frequent and intense. Forecasts and warnings need to be timely and specific to each region so that authorities, hospitals, farmers, and citizens can prepare.

Objectives

  • Analyse temperature patterns by region and season.
  • Forecast maximum temperature and classify heatwave severity.
  • Check forecasts against ground weather stations.
  • Share clear dashboards and advisories.

Five layers of the solution

  1. Data acquisition (IMD history, weather station readings)
  2. Data analytics (cleaning, region and season analysis)
  3. AI heatwave intelligence (forecast, hotspots, severity)
  4. Forecast validation with ground stations
  5. Decision support (dashboard, warnings, advisories)

What this website covers

This site is the decision-support layer. It uses sample data from js/data.js instead of a real forecasting model. Severity levels: Watch from 37°C, Heatwave from 40°C, Severe heatwave from 45°C (simplified for this project).

Tools and testing

HTML, CSS, and JavaScript only, with no libraries. Layout adapts to phone, tablet, and desktop. Keyboard and screen-reader friendly.

Sources & Stakeholders

This section provides information about the institutional partners, meteorological data sources, sensor networks, and agencies involved in the Heatwave Intelligence System.

Primary Meteorological & Sensor Sources

The platform aggregates real-time observational inputs from certified environmental monitoring grids to drive predictive algorithms.

India Meteorological Department (IMD): Provides primary synoptic weather feeds, historical climate baselines, and official regional warnings.

Automated Weather Station (AWS) Network: Delivers ground-level temperature, solar irradiance, and relative humidity telemetry updated every 15 minutes.

Satellite Radiometry Grids: Supplies land surface temperature (LST) mapping to detect urban heat island (UHI) concentrations.

Institutional & Action Stakeholders

State & District Disaster Management Authorities (SDMA/DDMA): Key operational nodes responsible for issuing localized heat alerts and managing emergency cooling centers.

Municipal Public Health Departments: Utilize telemetry diagnostics to coordinate hospital heat-stroke wards and dispatch mobile medical units.

Academic & Research Partners: Academic leadership led by the Department of Computer Engineering, K. J. Somaiya School of Engineering (Somaiya Vidyavihar University) for model refinement and architectural deployment.

Data Integrity Note: All external API feeds undergo automated validation checks prior to alert generation. Inquiries regarding data sharing protocols can be submitted via the Portal Directory.
